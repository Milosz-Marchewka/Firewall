Getting Started

  1. Open the Cyber game client in your browser.
  2. Sign in or choose a guest profile.
  3. Join the lobby and wait for a match.
  4. Once matched, the battle screen opens automatically.
Tip If the game doesn’t connect immediately, refresh once and check your network.

Accounts & Profiles

  • Registered account: Keep your progress and unlocks.
  • Guest account: Quick play, no long-term storage.

Use the account screen to log in, create a profile, or continue as guest.

Lobby & Rooms

The lobby is where you wait for a match or join a specific room. When another player joins, the match begins.

Room Actions

  • Join a room and wait for the opponent.
  • Ready up to start when prompted.
  • Leave the room if you want to cancel.

Battle Screen

The battle screen shows your character, the opponent, and the action bar. You’ll take turns selecting abilities.

UI AreaPurpose
Player PanelsHealth, buffs, and active status effects.
Ability BarCast skills and view cooldowns.
Log / EventsSee recent actions and results.

Abilities & Effects

Abilities have distinct effects such as damage, healing, freeze, and debuffs. Hover or open details to read the description.

Common Ability Types

  • Damage: Reduce opponent health.
  • Heal: Restore your health.
  • Freeze: Deny or delay opponent actions.
  • Utility: Buffs, debuffs, or special effects.

Status Effects

Status effects are shown on player panels. They can change damage, limit actions, or provide protection.

  • Frozen: Miss or delay a turn.
  • Boosted: Increased damage or healing.
  • Weakened: Reduced output or defense.

Skill Tree

The skill tree lets you unlock new abilities and passive bonuses. Spend points earned from matches to progress.

Tip Focus on one branch early to maximize synergy.

Tips & Strategies

  • Track cooldowns and plan around them.
  • Use freeze abilities to interrupt enemy combos.
  • Balance damage and defense to survive long matches.

Troubleshooting

Connection Issues

  • Refresh the page and rejoin the lobby.
  • Verify your internet connection is stable.

Match Not Starting

  • Leave and re-enter the room.
  • Check if the opponent disconnected.

FAQ

Does the game save progress? Yes, for registered accounts.

Can I play on mobile? The UI is responsive, but desktop is recommended.

How do I report a bug? Share a screenshot and describe the steps that caused it.
